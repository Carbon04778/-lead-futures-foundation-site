The Nervous System Leadership Initiative.

Self regulation and somatic wellbeing for the people who carry our communities, from firefighters and police officers to teachers, students, social workers, and frontline staff. Because confidence, belonging, and leadership have to be physiologically sustainable, not just aspirational.

The initiative is designed and led by Darlene Erich: a healthcare executive with more than twenty years leading critical care teams, a former Chief Nursing Officer, and a trauma informed somatic educator. She has spent a career inside the pressure her students live under, and she builds the training she wishes her own teams had been given.

Regulated Educators

Helping teachers and school staff stay grounded through the school day, so they leave with energy left over and can pass those same skills to their students.

Youth Labs

Teaching students to understand their own nervous system, a leadership skill for staying steady under pressure, in performance, and in conflict.

Social Impact Leaders

Supporting social workers and nonprofit staff who absorb other people's hardest days, with tools to recover, recharge, and keep helping for the long haul.

Resilient Responders

Giving firefighters, police officers, and first responders practical ways to stay calm in the moment, reset between calls, and lean on each other to make a hard job sustainable.

The founding circle

The Starting Lineup.

Nine positions. Ever. Named for the nine on a baseball field and anchored by Padres legend Steve Garvey as Captain, giving back to the city that cheered him. Membership is a distinction, not a transaction: the same names anchor the circle year after year.

Every invitation is an honor extended in the Captain's name. Founding gifts endow the Legacy Fund, principal preserved, programs funded from the returns, so your name works forever.

For Steve, it's simple: San Diego spent decades cheering for him, and this is how he cheers back.

01

A seat at the Captain's Table. The most exclusive room at every signature evening, reserved for founding members.

02

Your name on real outcomes. Sponsor the EVOKicks shoes given to students in your name: a gift tied to a child, not a balance sheet.

03

A permanent place in the story. Founding members are unveiled on stage at An Evening with Steve Garvey, and the roster never grows.

Why we exist

Born from three lives that gave to their community.

LEAD Futures was created in memory of three extraordinary people whose values became our foundation. Their Women in Tech scholarships, supporting women through four year college degrees, are the founding program of LEAD and the beginning of a broader commitment to advancing women in technology.

In memory

Kathleen Mito

A tenacious, kind, humble, and funny immigrant from Egypt who embraced every opportunity before her and never let go of her heritage. Often the only woman in the room, Kathleen never cowered, and always outstretched her hand to bring another woman to the table. Her scholarship carries that hand forward.

In memory

Jonathan Oliver

A young man whose genuine love and appreciation touched an extraordinary number of people, grounded in faith, gratitude, and service. Survived by two young nieces, Jonathan knew how important it was to create more opportunity for women in technology, and his scholarship does exactly that.

In memory

Kevin Buckley

One of the finest IT salespeople, Kevin effortlessly used his talents to mentor those new to the industry, cracking legendary "Kevin isms" along the way. His scholarship supports upcoming IT enthusiasts who want to make a tangible change, commemorating his infectious charisma and thirst for life.
